Chart & Key

PROTOTYPE — CLIENT DEMO
Demo only. This is a prototype backed by a free Firebase project. Records are locked with a 6-digit PIN — better than 4 digits, but still not real security or HIPAA/DPDP-grade protection. Don't enter real names, real Aadhaar digits, or real medical information.

Add a medical record

Fill this in once. You'll get a Patient ID to give your doctor, plus a 6-digit PIN — share the PIN separately, and only with the doctor you want to have access.

Look up a patient

Search by name to find the record, then enter the 6-digit PIN the patient gave you to unlock it.
